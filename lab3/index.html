Newton's Second Law cart track

A 500 g cart on a level track, pulled by a hanging mass over a pulley. One of you releases the cart, one times it with the stopwatch from release until the cart reaches the gate at 100 cm, and one records. Time every run three times. The friction-off prediction run is not hand-timed: you predict it first. The light gate's exact times stay hidden until your teacher shows them with a password. Gravity here is g = 9.8 m/s2.

Ready. Choose your masses, then release the cart. The hanging mass's drop is shortened so it fits on screen.
